## Remaining

Measured on `origin/main` at commit `8bd6632`.

- The storage policy of SUT-N-05 is only partly delivered, and in the application rather than in this package: `apps/web/src/screens/settings/storage-quota.ts` requests the persistence and reminds the user to export from 30 % of the quota (`shouldRemindExport`, with its own unit tests). The quota warning above 80 % and the 30-day export reminder were not implemented.
- The error paths have no test: `error.scenario-exists` (`packages/git-store/src/scenarios.ts`) is not covered, and neither are a quota exceeded, an unknown version or a stale canonical view.
- `readVersion` has no blob cache by oid: every read goes through `isomorphic-git`, which caches objects itself.
- The milestone tag is lightweight (`git.tag` without a message), not annotated.
- The cell-history test walks two versions rather than three.
- The export test does not skip when the `git` binary is unavailable; it fails instead.
