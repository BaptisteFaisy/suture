## Remaining

- `remapAndToA1` (`packages/formula/src/remap.ts`) was not written: the package offers `a1ToR1c1`, `r1c1ToA1` and `shiftA1`, and no `IndexMap` type exists. The combine engine therefore carries a formula in R1C1 — a position-independent notation — and `@suture/ooxml-patch` re-anchors it at the target cell when it writes the sheet (`packages/ooxml-patch/src/formula-input.ts`).
- The absolute remapping through an alignment map and the inserted-span verification flag (`toVerify`) were consequently not implemented.
- The upper Excel bound (row 1,048,576, column `XFD`) is not enforced: only a reference landing before row 1 or column 1 is reported and clamped.
