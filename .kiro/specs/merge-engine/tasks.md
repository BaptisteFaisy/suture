## Remaining

Measured on `origin/main` at commit `8bd6632`.

- The formula transfer (`packages/merge/src/formula-transfer.ts`, `remapAndToA1`) was not written: a carried formula stays in R1C1 — a position-independent notation — and `@suture/ooxml-patch` re-anchors it at the target cell when it writes the sheet (`packages/ooxml-patch/src/formula-input.ts`). The absolute remapping through an alignment map, the inserted-span check and the `toVerify` flag are therefore not implemented; the `to-review` report category exists in the contract but is never produced.
- The two-way degraded mode (no common origin: every difference becomes a disagreement) was not implemented. The application computes the common origin (`apps/web/src/store/ancestry.ts`) and refuses the combine with `error.no-common-origin` when the two histories are unrelated.
- There is no separate audit pass and no `SILENT_LOSS` error: the accounting is built into `planMerge` and verified by the property SUT-P-08 only.
- The unit tests do not cover a formula transfer with inserted rows, and they do not cover the degraded mode (which does not exist).
