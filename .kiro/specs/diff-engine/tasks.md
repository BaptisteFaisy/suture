## Remaining

Measured on `origin/main` at commit `8bd6632`.

- The 200,000-cell comparison benchmark (`packages/diff/test/bench.test.ts`, SUT-N-03) was not written: the diff package has no benchmark file, and no timing of a full-sheet comparison is recorded in the repository.
- The LCS alignment builds the full DP matrix instead of a rolling two-row DP, and neither the `4e8` cap nor the unique-signature anchor fallback were implemented: a comparison of two very large sheets is not protected against a memory blow-up.
- A rename is detected when the sheet id is stable; the content-overlap heuristic (a renamed sheet whose id also changed) was not implemented — such a sheet is reported as one removed sheet and one added sheet.
- The column alignment has no feature flag, and its signatures are computed on all the columns of the sheet rather than on the paired rows only.
- The unit tests of this package do not assert the exact 50% half-overlap boundary, the R1C1 shift invariance of a comparison, or the deterministic ordering of the result.
